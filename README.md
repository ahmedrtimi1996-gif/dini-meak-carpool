# DiniM3ak: Take Me With You

Build a production-ready carpooling marketplace named "DiniM3ak".

Brand Identity

Name: DiniM3ak

Meaning:

DiniM3ak means "Take me with you" in Moroccan Darija. The platform connects drivers with empty seats and passengers traveling in the same direction across Morocco.

Mission:

Provide safe, affordable and reliable carpooling for everyone in Morocco.

Target users:

- Daily commuters

- University students

- Professionals

- Families

- Tourists

Primary market:

Morocco

Future expansion:

North Africa and Europe

Brand values:

- Trust

- Safety

- Simplicity

- Community

- Sustainability

Color palette:

Primary: #0F766E (Emerald Green)

Secondary: #F59E0B (Amber)

Accent: #2563EB (Blue)

Background: White and light gray

Typography:

Modern, clean, premium.

Logo concept:

A modern location pin combined with a road and a car, including the text "DiniM3ak".

Slogan:

"Voyagez ensemble, économisez davantage."

Alternative slogans:

- Votre trajet commence ici.

- Ensemble vers la même destination.

- Le covoiturage intelligent au Maroc.

- Partagez la route, partagez les économies.

The website must have a premium design inspired by Uber, Airbnb and BlaBlaCar, while remaining completely original.

Languages:

- French

- Arabic (RTL)

- English

Currency:

Moroccan Dirham (MAD)

Date format:

DD/MM/YYYY

Time format:

24 hours

Default country:

Morocco
